## At a glance

Txtudio Coretech Private Limited is a product company in Kozhikode, Kerala, incorporated on 31 October 2025. I founded it with Arun Rajanikanth and Devang C V and lead it as CEO and Managing Director. We build four products: Workeron, a business suite for HR, CRM, project management, marketing and forms; Snaptiqz for event ticketing; DoYourDesign for interior design studios; and the Audit Management Solution for Indian banks.

## Why Txtudio exists

Txtudio started with events, then moved into HR, interior design studios and bank audit. The work began before the company did: our GitHub organisation was created in May 2025 and snaptiqz.com was registered in July 2025.

Our About page calls each of these areas operational work that "runs on spreadsheets and goodwill until someone builds it properly". The site names what three of the products replace:

- Workeron replaces registers, sheets and WhatsApp groups.
- Snaptiqz replaces Google Forms, cash counters and paper lists.
- The Audit Management Solution replaces paper audit files, spreadsheets and emailed compliance.

Our tagline sums it up: software for the work that can't go wrong. What we build is often the record someone else will check. So it has to show where a number came from and who changed it.

## Four products, one standard

Each product does one job well, for one kind of team. We would rather go deep on one kind of work than cover every kind of work thinly. The standard the products share is written down as four principles:

- **Focused products.** One job, one kind of team.
- **Scoped by role.** Everyone sees exactly what their role allows, and nothing more.
- **Built to be audited.** Actions are logged, data has lineage and access is deliberate.
- **Made in Kozhikode, used everywhere.** All four products are built and supported by our team in Kozhikode.

### Workeron Suite

Workeron is a business suite: HRM, CRM, project management, marketing and forms, several business products on one sign-in, for anything from a single office to many branches. In its HRM, attendance is geo-fenced, so staff can check in only inside their branch's fence. Leave and approvals, worklogs, a document vault with expiry tracking and reports complete the HR side.

- **Built for:** businesses and their teams, from a single office to many branches.
- **On record:** the [Workeron HRM app](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=app.workeron.hrm) is on Google Play under the developer name Txtudio Coretech.
- **Website:** [workeron.app](https://workeron.app)

### Snaptiqz

Snaptiqz is our event ticketing and QR check-in platform, and it was our first product line. Organisers sell free or paid tickets, run registration forms with approvals and check people in with a phone as the scanner. One account also covers email and WhatsApp campaigns, a payouts ledger and certificates for checked-in attendees.

- **Built for:** event organisers, from educational institutions and student communities to professional associations, corporates, conference and cultural organisers, non-profits, and sports and competition organisers.
- **On record:** pricing is per ticket, with custom plans for larger events.
- **Website:** [snaptiqz.com](https://snaptiqz.com)

### DoYourDesign

DoYourDesign runs an interior design studio from one place. Its nine modules include projects, snags, site visits, milestones, a material library, ledger and margins, and quotations and invoices. Every project moves through four stages: Design, Execution, Handover and Completed.

- **Built for:** interior design studios in India, from a principal designer with a small team to multi-site studios.
- **On record:** quotations and GST invoices apply CGST and SGST or IGST by place of supply, under GST Regular or Composition.
- **Website:** [doyourdesign.com](https://doyourdesign.com)

### Audit Management Solution

The Audit Management Solution is on-premise internal audit software for Indian banks. It handles risk-based internal audit (RBIA) alongside concurrent, statutory, IS and credit audits, plus offsite monitoring and optional AML transaction monitoring. Each bank gets its own install, with no shared tenancy.

- **Built for:** internal audit, risk and compliance teams at scheduled commercial, co-operative, small finance and regional rural banks, and at PACS and co-operative societies.
- **On record:** a read-only connector reads the bank's core banking system and never writes to it, and the interface is in English and Malayalam.
- **Website:** [txtudio.com/products/audit-management](https://www.txtudio.com/products/audit-management)
