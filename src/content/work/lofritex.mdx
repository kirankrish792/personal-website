## At a glance

Lofritex IT Solutions LLP is a custom software and ERP studio in Kozhikode, Kerala, incorporated on 19 July 2024. I lead it as CEO and one of its founders. We build web, mobile and ERP software for clients in India, Saudi Arabia, Sweden and Dubai, plus our own Manage Matey ERP.

## Why Lofritex exists

Lofritex is the older of my two companies. The name stands for our core values, Loyalty, Friendship, Technology and Expertise, and our tagline is "Your Vision. Our Expertise."

Our work is software shaped around how a client already works. "Tailored solutions, not templates" is the first benefit on our services page, and we take projects from concept to launch.

We also try to keep what we ship light. Everything online consumes energy, so we build lean and target performance scores of 90 and above.

### Manage Matey ERP

Manage Matey is our own ERP product, at [managematey.com](https://managematey.com). It brings CRM, HRM, billing, finance, orders and inventory into one system.

It grew out of client work. For a contractor in Saudi Arabia, we built an ERP covering employee management, mobilisation tracking, purchase orders, invoices and quotations. Our three-part series on that project, posted on [our LinkedIn page](https://www.linkedin.com/company/lofritex-it-solutions/), ends with the system becoming the core of Manage Matey.

## How we use AI

In April 2026 I wrote [AI Isn't a Shortcut, It's Our 11th Team Member](https://lofritex.com/blog/ai-isnt-a-shortcut-its-our-11th-team-member/) for the Lofritex blog. The post describes us as a lean team of 10 and treats AI as the eleventh member rather than a shortcut. It sets out four habits:

- **Loud AI.** Share the prompts you use. If I use a prompt to refactor code or summarise a client's messy requirements, I share that prompt.
- **Radical data hygiene.** Clean input, reliable output.
- **Method sharing over status meetings.** We cut status-update meetings and share methods instead.
- **Fix-it Fridays, the AI edition.** Once a month we hold a 90-minute session, and we leave the room with a fix, not a to-do list.

How I direct coding agents myself is on my [About page](/about).
