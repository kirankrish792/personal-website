## Open source and community

In 2021 I ran a Git, HTML, CSS and JavaScript task series for students through the IEEE Computer Society chapter at College of Engineering Vadakara. The seven task repositories are still public on the chapter's [GitHub organisation](https://github.com/IEEE-CS-SBC-CEV).

In Hacktoberfest 2021 I opened 13 pull requests in two days, and one, a polygon pattern in Python, was merged. Since 2025 most of my code has lived in private repositories, so my public [GitHub profile](https://github.com/kirankrish792) mostly shows earlier work.
