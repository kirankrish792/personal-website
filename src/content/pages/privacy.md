This site sets no cookies, has no forms and never asks for your name or email address. It counts visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies. I see totals, such as page views and referring sites, not individual visitors.

Your browser keeps one localStorage entry for your theme and motion preferences: light or dark mode, and whether you paused the animation. A session-only flag also stops the opening animation from replaying, and it clears when you close the tab. Both stay on your device, and clearing this site's data removes them.

Booking a call takes you to a separate scheduling service, which has its own privacy terms. If you have questions about any of this, ask me through the [Contact page](/contact).
